<KeyIdea>
Play do alag kism ke niyam manwata hai. **Tareekhein** — target API ka star — tay hain, saal bhar pehle
chhapi jati hain, aur aapko updates bhejne se rok deti hain. **Ghoshnayein** — data safety, permissions ka
istemal — usse milakar dekhi jati hain jo aapka app sach mein karta hai, aur bemel mahinon baad kisi
karyavahi ki shakal mein saamne aata hai.
</KeyIdea>

## Target API wali tareekh

```kotlin title="build.gradle.kts" numbered
android {
    compileSdk = 36
    defaultConfig {
        minSdk = 24        // aapka chunaav: kitna purana device aap sambhalte ho
        targetSdk = 35     // Play ka niyam: kitne naye behaviour ko aapne mana
    }
}
```

`targetSdk` batata hai ki aapne platform ke kaun se behaviour ke hisab se khud ko dhala hai — peeche
chalne ki hadein, permissions ki vyavastha, storage ke niyam. Play chahta hai ki wo taza release ke ek saal
ke andar ho, aur wo tareekh har August khiskati hai:

| Mote taur par | Shart |
| --- | --- |
| Har saal, ~August | Naye apps aur updates ko pichhle saal ka API star lena padta hai |
| Usse neeche pade maujooda apps | Updates nahi bhej sakte; lage reh sakte hain par naye devices ke naye users ko dikhte nahi |

Jo baat log chook jate hain wo ye hai ki tareekh chookne par aapka app hatta nahi — wo jam jata hai. Aap
koi sudhaar, koi suraksha ka paiband, kuch bhi nahi bhej sakte jab tak `targetSdk` na badhao aur us platform
ki version ne jo badla use sambhal na lo. Isiliye tareekh se ek hafte pehle tak taala gaya wo badlaav us
kaam ka sabse bura roop hai.

`minSdk` ka neeti se koi lena-dena nahi aur wo poori tarah aapka faisla hai ki kaun se devices sambhalne
hain. Use badhana users chhodta hai; ghatana anukoolta ka kaam badhata hai.

## Wo ghoshnayein jinhe mel khana hai

| Ghoshna | Play kisse milakar dekhta hai |
| --- | --- |
| Data safety | Aapka app aur uski SDKs sach mein kya jama aur share karti hain |
| Permissions | Koi samvedansheel permission kisi mukhya suvidha se mel khati hai ya nahi |
| Lakshit log | App bachchon ke liye bana hai ya nahi, jisse niyam badal jate hain |
| Vigyapan | App mein ads hain ya nahi, jisse jagahein badalti hain |

**Samvedansheel permissions** ko aisa ghoshit istemal chahiye jo mukhya suvidha ho, suvidhajanak hona nahi.
`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`, saari files ki pahunch, SMS tatha call log ki pahunch, peeche wali location aur pakke
alarms, sabko form chahiye aur zyadatar maangein thukra di jati hain — aur yahi platform wale lessons ki
baat neeti bankar aati hai: agar koi sankri API maujood hai, to chaudi permission manzoor nahi hogi.

**Khata mitana** ab zaruri hai: jis app mein khata banta hai use app ke andar mitane ka raasta aur uske liye
ek web ka URL dena padta hai. Wo kagzi nahi, banane ka kaam hai, aur teams ko der se pakadta hai.

<Detail summary="Karyavahi wali email ka matlab asal mein kya hai">
Play ki karyavahiyon mein darje hain, aur aapko kaun si mili ye janna jawab badal deta hai:

- **Chetavani** — tareekh wali neeti ki baat. Theek karke dobara bhejiye; abhi kuch band nahi hua.
- **Asweekriti** — yahi ek build thukraya gaya. Chal rahe roop par koi asar nahi.
- **Hataya jana** — Listing khoj aur install se gayab. Maujooda users ke paas app rehta hai.
- **Nilamban** — App gaya aur developer ka khata khatre mein.
- **Khata khatam** — Har app, hamesha ke liye, aur Google naya khata banane nahi deta.

Maayne ye chadhai rakhti hai: baar-baar ke ullanghan aapko upar ki seedhi par le jate hain, to chetavani ko
vaikalpik manna hi wo tarika hai jisse koi team alag-alag dekhne par chhoti si baat ke liye nilamban tak
pahunch jati hai.

Appeal ki vidhi asli hai aur jab aapko lage ki faisla galat hai tab lene layak hai, par wo seemit baatcheet
wala ek form hai. Madad byora karta hai: us SDK ka naam lijiye, wo code uddhrit kijiye, wo screen jodiye.
"Hum aisa karte hi nahi" wali aam appeal shayad hi chalti hai, kyunki sameekshak us behaviour ko dekh raha
hai jo usne dekha aur use usse milane ke liye kuch thos chahiye.
</Detail>

## Aam galtiyan

<Pitfall title="Paanch, aur pehli aapka app jama deti hai">
**`targetSdk` badhana tareekh tak taalna.** Jab tak wo na ho, aap kuch bhi nahi bhej sakte.

**Sirf apna jama karna ghoshit karna.** Play us app ko parakhta hai, SDKs samet.

**Sankri API hote hue chaudi permission maangna.** Wo ghoshna wala form use bachayega nahi.

**Chetavani andekhi karna.** Ullanghan chadhte hain; chetavani kaam karne ka sabse sasta mauka hai.

**App ke andar khata mitane ka raasta na hona.** Khaton wale har app ke liye zaruri, aur wo banane ka kaam
hai.
</Pitfall>

## Abhyaas

1. Apna `targetSdk` Play ki maujooda shart se milaiye aur agli tareekh likhiye.
2. Har dependency aur wo kya jama karti hai, ginaiye; apne Data safety ke form se milaiye.
3. Apne manifest mein koi samvedansheel permission dhoondhiye aur wo sankri API pehchaniye jo aap le sakte
   the.
4. Play ki khata mitane wali shart padhiye aur dekhiye ki aapka app us par khara utarta hai ya nahi.
5. `targetSdk` ek badhaiye aur jo toote use theek kijiye, zaruri banne se pehle.

<Interview title="Ek sawal, aisi ghoshna ka jiske liye kisi ne code likha hi nahi">
"Data safety ka ullanghan aap kaise bachaoge?"

Apna code dekhne ke bajaye dependencies ginakar, kyunki Play us app ko parakhta hai aur zyadatar jama karna
un SDKs se aata hai jinhe kisi ne likha hi nahi. Ads wali SDK banawat se hi vigyapan ki id jama karti hai,
attribution wali SDK hoti hi jama aur share karne ke liye hai, aur Firebase Analytics kai doosri Firebase
wali libraries ke saath apne aap aati hai aur turant jama karna shuru kar deti hai. Koi team apne likhe ke
baare mein poori tarah imandaar ho sakti hai aur phir bhi uski ghoshna mel na khaye.

To padtal hai har dependency ki list, har ek kya jama karna batati hai, aur un sabka jod ghoshit karna. Uske
saath main wo sharten rakhunga jo kagaz nahi, banane ka kaam hain — khaton wale har app ke liye app ke andar
khata mitana, aur pahunch mein privacy policy ka URL — aur kisi bhi chetavani ko kaam karne ka mauka
manunga, kyunki ullanghan chadhte hain aur chetavani us seedhi ki sabse sasti paydan hai.
</Interview>
