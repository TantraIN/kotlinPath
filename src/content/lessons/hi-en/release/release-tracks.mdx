<KeyIdea>
Dheere-dheere failana ye seemit karta hai ki **koi kharab release kitne users tak pahunche** — par sirf
tab tak jab tak aap aisa koi aankda dekh rahe ho jo bata de ki wo kharab hai, aur sirf tab jab rokna aisa
faisla ho jo koi jaldi le sake.
</KeyIdea>

## Tracks

| Track | Kise milta hai | Sameeksha |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internal | Aapke ginaye 100 tak dekhne wale | Minute; lagbhag koi nahi |
| Closed | Koi list ya Google Group | Poori sameeksha |
| Open | Jo bhi listing se jud jaye | Poori sameeksha |
| Production | Sab | Poori sameeksha |

Internal wala track lagatar lene layak hai, kyunki wo minton mein mil jata hai aur use sameeksha nahi
chahiye. Closed wo jagah hai jahan asli beta wala samooh rehta hai. Open sarvajanik hai aur aapki listing
par dikhta hai, to wo padtal jitna hi marketing ka faisla hai.

Sameeksha ke baare mein do baatein. Usmein aam taur par ghanton se ek din lagta hai, par pehli baar bhejne
ya kisi samvedansheel permission ko chhoone wale app mein hafta bhi lag sakta hai — to release ke calendar
mein uski jagah honi chahiye. Aur sameeksha har track par alag hoti hai, to jo build kisi track par
sameeksha paar kar chuka hai use aage badhana tez hai.

**Pre-launch ki reportein** aapke build ko Google ki prayogshala mein asli devices par chalati hain aur
kisi ke lagane se pehle crashes, sulabhta ki mushkilein tatha performance ki baatein batati hain. Wo muft
hain aur zyadatar teams unhe andekha karti hain.

## Dheere-dheere failana

```text title="Aam chadhai" numbered
din 0   1%     crash-free dar aur ANR ki dar dekhiye
din 1   5%     agar thehra hua ho
din 2   20%
din 4   50%
din 7   100%
```

Pratishat se kam maayne wo thehrav rakhte hain. Jo failav ek ghante mein 1% se 100% pahunch jaye wo failav
nahi hai; wo der se kiya gaya poora release hai.

Dekhne ki list chhoti hai: crash se bache users, ANR ki dar, aur ek aisa behaviour wala aankda jo kisi kaam
ke tootne ko pakad le — har ghante sign-ins, har ghante orders, aapka app jo bhi karta hai. Akeli crash-free
dar us release ko chook jati hai jismein kuch crash nahi hota aur checkout ka button kuch karta hi nahi.

## Rokna, aur wo ektarfa darwaza

```text title="Kya ho sakta hai aur kya nahi" numbered
failav rokna            -> haan, turant; naye users ko milna band
failav phir shuru karna -> haan
failav wapas lena       -> nahi
```

Bheji hui cheez wapas lene jaisa kuch hai hi nahi. Rokna us release ko aur users tak pahunchne se rokta hai,
par jo pehle hi update kar chuke unke paas wahi toota hua roop rehta hai — lage hue app ko neeche utarne ka
Android mein koi raasta nahi. Aage ka iklauta ilaaj bade `versionCode` wala naya build hai, jise sameeksha
chahiye.

Iske do natije hain. Pehla, wo chadhai itni dheemi honi chahiye ki rokna sach mein nuksan ghataye; 50% par
rokna aapke aadhe users bachata hai, jo sunne mein jitna lagta hai usse kahin kam hai. Doosra, jis cheez ko
band karna pad sakta hai wo gunvatta wale phase ke Remote Config ke flag ke peeche honi chahiye — un users
tak pahunchne ki iklauti vyavastha wahi hai jo pehle hi update kar chuke, aur wo dinon ke bajaye minton mein
chalti hai.

<Detail summary="Managed publishing, aur release ka calendar">
**Managed publishing** sameeksha ko release se alag kar deta hai: builds sameeksha se gujarkar manzoor hue
pade rehte hain jab tak aap publish na dabao. Uske bina, manzoor hua build tabhi chala jata hai jab
sameeksha khatam hoti hai, jo shanivar ki raat do baje bhi ho sakti hai.

Ye isliye maayne rakhta hai ki gadbad hoti kab hai. Shukrawar dopahar nikli release ke paas poora weekend hai
ek-sitara sameekshayein jama karne ke liye, isse pehle ki koi aankde padhe, aur wo ek-sitara sameekshayein us
bug se zyada jeeti hain — Play ki rating judti jati hai, to ek kharab weekend mahinon tak ank dabaye rakhta
hai.

Jis vyavharik shakal par zyadatar teams pahunchti hain:

- Somwar se budhwar, subah mein bhejiye, aur koi dekh raha ho.
- Internal track lagatar; production tay lay par.
- Aisa ek naamzad insaan jo bina baithak ke rok sake.
- Wapsi ki yojana likhi hui, jo Remote Config ka flag hai, naya build nahi.

Ismein kuch bhi takniki nahi hai, aur ye sab milkar tay karta hai ki dheere-dheere failana asal mein kitne
kaam ka hai.
</Detail>

## Aam galtiyan

<Pitfall title="Paanch, aur doosri failane ko bematlab kar deti hai">
**Sirf crash-free dar dekhna.** Bina crash ke toota bahav use dikhta hi nahi.

**Bina dekhe failana.** 100% par bhejne jaisa hi anjaam, kuch aur kadmon ke saath.

**Wapas lene ki ummid.** Aap nahi le sakte; jinhone update kiya unke paas toota build rehta hai.

**Shukrawar ko release.** Weekend ek-sitara sameekshaon se bhar jata hai jo sudhaar se zyada jeeti hain.

**Pre-launch ki reportein andekhi karna.** Asli devices par crashes aur sulabhta ki muft baatein.
</Pitfall>

## Abhyaas

1. Internal wale track par bhejiye aur likhiye ki milne mein kitni der lagti hai.
2. 1% wala failav shuru kijiye, phir rokiye, aur pakka kijiye ki jo pehle update kar chuke unka kya hota hai.
3. Crash-free dar ke saath apne release ke dashboard par behaviour wala ek aankda jodiye.
4. Apne maujooda build ki pre-launch ki report padhiye.
5. Agli release se pehle apni sabse jokhim bhari nai suvidha Remote Config ke flag ke peeche rakhiye.

<Interview title="Ek sawal, ki failav aapko bachata kisse hai">
"Koi jokhim bhara badlaav aap kaise bhejoge?"

Pehle Remote Config ke flag ke peeche, band par default rakhkar, taki wo code nishkriya jaye aur main use
bina release chalu kar sakun — aur gadbad hone par minton mein band. Ye isliye maayne rakhta hai ki
dheere-dheere failana wapas nahi liya ja sakta: rokna naye users tak pahunchna band karta hai, par jo pehle
hi update kar chuke unke paas wahi rehta hai, aur Android mein neeche utarne ka raasta hai hi nahi.

Phir dheemi chadhai — 1%, 5%, 20%, asli thehravon ke saath — aur koi sach mein dekhta hua. Dekhne ki list
mein behaviour wala aankda hona hi chahiye, sirf crash-free dar aur ANR nahi, kyunki jis nakami ko pakadne ke
liye failav hai wo aksar wahi hai jahan kuch crash nahi hota aur koi button chupchap kuch nahi karta. Aur
main hafte ke shuru mein, subah bhejunga, kyunki shukrawar ki release weekend bhar ek-sitara sameekshayein
jama karti hai aur Play ki rating judti jati hai — bug theek ho jata hai aur ank mahinon tak nahi ubarta.
</Interview>
