<KeyIdea>
**AAB lagaya nahi ja sakta**. Wo aapke app ka byora hai jisse Play har device ke liye APK banata hai — ek
density, ek ABI, ek bhasha. To jo cheez users ko milti hai wo aapke bundle se chhoti hai aur usse alag hai
jo aapne sthaniya roop se banaya.
</KeyIdea>

## AAB banam APK

```bash title="Build" numbered
./gradlew bundleRelease            # app-release.aab  -> yahi chadhaiye
./gradlew assembleRelease          # app-release.apk  -> sirf sthaniya jaanch ke liye
```

Play par naye apps ke liye App Bundles 2021 se zaruri hain. Bachat asli hai: kisi device ko har variant ke
bajaye har ek ka ek hi milta hai, jo kai densities aur ABIs wale app mein download ko aam taur par
ek-tihai ya usse zyada ghata deta hai.

Baat wahi hai jo logon ko pakadta hai: aap AAB laga nahi sakte, aur jo APK aapne sthaniya roop se banai
wo alag key se hastaksharit hai aur usmein har variant hai. User ko sach mein jo milta hai use dekhne ke
liye:

```bash title="bundletool" numbered
bundletool build-apks --bundle=app-release.aab --output=app.apks \
  --ks=upload.jks --ks-key-alias=upload --local-testing
bundletool install-apks --apks=app.apks
```

Ya Play Console ke App Bundle Explorer se hastaksharit, device wali APK utariye, jo Play ne theek-theek
kya banaya ye dekhne ka iklauta raasta hai.

## Version

```kotlin title="Versions" numbered
defaultConfig {
    versionCode = 42            // ek purnank jise Play milata hai; hamesha badhna chahiye
    versionName = "2.3.1"       // users ko dikhti string; Play ko isse matlab nahi
}
```

Play sirf `versionCode` manwata hai, aur niyam pakka hai: wo us track par aapke kabhi chadhaye har build se
bada hona chahiye, un builds samet jinhe aapne wapas liya ya mitaya. Dohraya gaya code thukra diya jata
hai, to use kisi lagatar badhti cheez se nikalna — CI ke chalne ki ginti, ya tareekh wala koi dhancha —
haath ki galti bacha leta hai.

App Bundle andar se alag codes wali kai APKs bhi bana sakta hai; aapka rakha `versionCode` buniyad hai, aur
baaki Play sambhal leta hai.

## Store ki listing

Listing kagzi karyavahi nahi, badlaav ki satah hai:

| Khana | Kya maayne rakhta hai |
| --- | --- |
| Naam (30 akshar) | Naam, aur agar jagah bache to ye ki wo hai kya |
| Chhota byora (80) | Khoj ke natijon mein dikhti wahi ek line |
| Poora byora (4000) | Padhi ja sakne wali bhasha; keywords thoosna neeti ka ullanghan hai |
| Screenshots | Pehle do hi zyadatar faisla dhote hain |
| Feature graphic | Listing par aur kuch jagahon par dikhta hai |

Screenshots wo khana hai jiska asar sabse bada hai aur jis par mehnat sabse kam lagti hai. Device ki saadi
tasveerein un framed screenshots se kam chalti hain jin par chhoti si likhai batati hai ki wo screen karti
kya hai, kyunki listing secondon mein scroll ho jati hai aur bina likhai wala screenshot kuch kehta hi
nahi.

Play **store listing ke prayog** bhi karne deta hai — icon, screenshots ya byore par A/B padtal, jo install
ki dar par naapi jati hai. Kaunsa screenshot zyada achha hai, is par andaruni raay se wo sach ka behtar
thikana hai.

<Detail summary="Data safety, jo aisi ghoshna hai jisse aapko sach mein mel khana hai">
Data safety wala form puchta hai ki aap kya jama karte ho, use share karte ho ya nahi, aur wo taar par
encrypt hota hai ya nahi. Wo aupcharikta nahi hai: Play use milakar dekhta hai, aur us ghoshna tatha aapki
SDKs ke sach mein karne ke beech ka bemel aam asweekriti bhi hai aur aam hataya jana bhi.

Jo hissa teams galat karti hain wo hai doosron ke jariye hota jama karna. Analytics, ads, crash ki
reporting aur attribution wali SDKs, sab apne aap jama karti hain, to imandaar ghoshna wo dhakti hai jo
aapki dependencies karti hain, sirf aapka apna code nahi. Firebase wale lesson ki baat seedhe lagu hai:
Analytics kai doosri SDKs ke saath aati hai aur turant jama karna shuru kar deti hai, to wo us form mein
jati hai, chahe aapne use chuna ho ya na ho.

Saath ki shart hai **privacy policy ka URL**, jo har us app ke liye zaruri hai jo kuch bhi jama karta hai —
aur asal mein har app karta hai, kyunki crash ki reporting device ki koi pehchan jama karti hai.

Ise galat karna build waqt ya sameeksha waqt bharose se nakaam nahi hota; wo mahinon baad kisi karyavahi ki
shakal mein saamne aata hai, aur isiliye ye ghoshna ek baar dhyan se karne layak hai.
</Detail>

## Aam galtiyan

<Pitfall title="Paanch, aur pehli ka matlab hai ki aapne galat cheez dekhi">
**Sarvabhaumik APK dekhna.** Users ko ek hissa milta hai; chalte waqt ki khoj usmein gayab ho sakti hai.

**Dohraya gaya `versionCode`.** Thukra diya jata hai, un builds ke saamne bhi jo aapne mita diye.

**Device ki saadi screenshots.** Listing ka sabse asardar khana, jis par aam taur par sabse kam kaam hota
hai.

**Byore mein keywords thoosna.** Neeti ka ullanghan hai, badhne ki tarkeeb nahi.

**Aisa Data safety ka form jo aapki SDKs chhod deta hai.** Play milakar dekhta hai; karyavahi baad mein
aati hai.
</Pitfall>

## Abhyaas

1. AAB banaiye aur use seedhe lagane ki koshish kijiye.
2. `bundletool` se device wali APKs banaiye aur naap ko sarvabhaumik APK se milaiye.
3. Pehle istemal kiya `versionCode` lekar chadhaiye aur asweekriti padhiye.
4. App Bundle Explorer se device wali APK utariye aur uska hastakshar dekhiye.
5. Apne app ki har SDK ginaiye aur har ek ko apni Data safety ki ghoshna se milaiye.

<Interview title="Ek sawal, galat cheez dekhne ka">
"Aapne release ki APK dekhi aur phir bhi users ko resources gayab mile. Kaise?"

Kyunki jo APK aapne banai wo sarvabhaumik hai aur jo users ko milti hai wo nahi. `assembleRelease` ek hi
cheez banata hai jismein har density, ABI aur bhasha hoti hai, to chalte waqt dhoondhi jane wali har cheez
— `getIdentifier` se dhoondha gaya koi drawable, path se ladi gayi koi native library — use mil jati hai.
Play us bundle ko baantkar har device ko ek hissa bhejta hai, aur us hisse mein wo cheez ho hi na, jiske
liye wo khoj haath badha rahi thi.

To main banai hui cheez ke bajaye bheji jane wali cheez dekhunga: `bundletool build-apks --local-testing`,
ya App Bundle Explorer se device wali APK utarkar, jo users ko sach mein dikhte hastakshar ko dekhne ka bhi
iklauta raasta hai. Aur main chalte waqt ki resource wali khoj ko R8 ke neeche reflection jaisi hi kism ki
mushkil manunga — aisi cheez jo auzar ko dikhti nahi, to use ya to dikhana padega ya hatana.
</Interview>
